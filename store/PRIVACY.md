# Privacy policy

Wetterkurve does not collect, transmit to its author, sell, or share personal
data. It makes direct HTTPS requests from the user's computer to Open-Meteo:

- A location-search query is sent only when the user types a location into the
  location picker.
- The latitude, longitude, and time zone of the selected location are sent to
  retrieve its weather forecast.

Saved locations are stored locally in GNOME settings. Wetterkurve has no own
server, analytics, advertising, account system, or API key.

Open-Meteo's own service and privacy terms apply to requests made to its API.
