# Wetterkurve

## Title
Wetterkurve

## Short description
A 3-day weather chart widget. Temperature, rain, optional cloud and wind.

## Full description
Wetterkurve puts a clear three-day forecast chart on your home screen.

The large widget shows temperature and feels-like, rain chance, rain amount, and weekdays. Cloud cover and wind can be toggled without shrinking the curve. The thin temperature bar shows current conditions and the place in a single row.

English is the default language, with German available in the app.

Save up to three locations and switch them in the app. The forecast refreshes about every 20 minutes, or immediately with ↻.

Weather and location search come from Open-Meteo. No account, no API key, no ads.
